      We’ll email a structured file (JSON + CSV) to the address on your account within 30 days, usually the same day.

      Automated decisions. Fraud checks may decline an order automatically. You can ask for a human review; Law 25 requires us to tell you this and give you the route. Request a review · Privacy officer: privacy@vanstro.ca, Vanstro Global Supply Inc., 200-XXXX Winnipeg, MB.

      Delete account

      Deleting removes your profile, addresses, saved lists and payment methods. Order records and consent records are kept: tax law requires us to keep invoices for 6 years (CRA), and CASL requires us to prove past consent. Nothing else survives. This can’t be undone.
