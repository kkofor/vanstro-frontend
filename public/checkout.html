How your payment and data are protected

Card payments

Payments are processed by Moneris Solutions Corporation (Toronto, ON), a PCI DSS Level 1 service provider. The card number, expiry date and security code field is served directly by Moneris (Hosted Tokenization) inside this page. Vanstro’s servers never receive, log or store them; our compliance scope is PCI DSS SAQ A. Moneris replaces your card details with a single-use temporary token, which our server sends to Moneris (server-to-server) to take the payment.

Apple Pay & Google Pay

Wallet payments are enabled in the Moneris Checkout profile (Payment → Accepted digital wallets). Apple Pay and Google Pay tokenize the card on the device; Moneris decrypts the token. Vanstro never sees a card number. Apple Pay requires your domain to be registered with Apple via Moneris; Google Pay requires HTTPS.

Saved cards

If you tick “Save this card”, Moneris stores the card in its Vault and gives Vanstro an opaque token plus the brand, last 4 digits and expiry. You can delete saved cards any time in My account › Payment methods; deleting removes the Vault record at Moneris.

Bank verification (3-D Secure 2)

Your card issuer may ask you to confirm a purchase in its own window. That exchange is between you and your bank; Vanstro only learns whether it succeeded.

Encryption & storage

All traffic uses TLS 1.3 with HSTS. Card numbers stay in the Moneris iframe and never reach Vanstro.

Privacy (PIPEDA · Quebec Law 25)

We collect only what we need to fulfil the order. Tax invoices are kept 6 years (CRA). You can export or delete other data in your account. Marketing email needs a separate opt-in (CASL).

Consumer protection

Before you pay we show the full price in CAD including taxes and delivery, the delivery estimate and our return policy, and give you the chance to correct errors (Ontario CPA 2002 s. 38, Quebec CPA s. 54.4 and equivalents). A copy of your order is emailed immediately after payment.
